# China/Hong Kong IC Job Sources

This list is focused on official company career pages for international and major China semiconductor/EDA employers with possible China/Hong Kong/US IC design, digital design, analog/mixed-signal, verification, physical design, architecture, or EDA roles.

`role_kind` is assigned by the bot per posting:

- `internship`: title/description contains internship, intern, co-op, student, or Chinese internship terms.
- `full_time`: title/description contains engineer, designer, new grad, campus, graduate, or Chinese campus/new-grad terms.
- `unknown`: the page/posting does not expose enough text; keep it for manual review.
